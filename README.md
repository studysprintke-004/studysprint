# studysprint
StudySprint is an all-in-one academic mobile companion app for university students in Kenya, providing access to study notes, past papers, reference books, and story books.
# Supported Universities
Egerton University
Kenyatta University
Chuka University
Karatina University
Maseno University
Moi University
Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT)
University of Nairobi (UON)
# App Features
Access to academic study notes
Revision past papersReference books and story booksMobile-friendly platform designed for university revision.
